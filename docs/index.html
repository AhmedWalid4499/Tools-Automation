Everything you do in GOLD, faster

GOLD Express signs in to GOLD for you (Orange single sign-on) and works in the background. Here is what you get.

My Orders

A dashboard of the orders GOLD lists as yours.

  • Counters: open, due in 30 days, late
  • Orders by status and customer
  • Customer, site reference, status, target date
  • Export to Excel

Order Assignment

Which APM has which open APM order - live from the daily APM Open Orders sheet.

  • Assigned, unassigned and "not in the DPM list"
  • Filter by region (Europe, APAC, AME) or name
  • Excel dashboard with charts

Overview & Tracker

The key facts of any orders, with a clear delivery status.

  • Red / Amber / Green vs the target date
  • 120-day lead time and order value
  • A tracker for management or customers, with your comments - ready to send in Excel

Order explorer

Open an order and browse every tab in neat tables.

  • Milestones, teams, pricing, notes
  • Screenshots and document downloads
  • Copy any table into Excel

The Friendly GOLD bot

Ask in plain English: "What are the deadlines?", "Who is the STM?", "Which of my orders are late?"

  • Reads GOLD live and answers in seconds
  • Tables and lists, not walls of text
  • Private: nothing kept by the AI

Bulk updates

Change many orders in one go.

  • Revised Target Date for a whole list (any date format)
  • Bulk assignment of the primary STM
  • Paste from Excel, Test-only mode, never saves the same order twice

Org Chart & DPM names

Find anyone at Orange Business and who they report to.

  • Email, phone, manager chain
  • The team of the order you have open
  • APM teams filled in automatically

Little things that help

Made for working every day.

  • Windows notifications for your orders
  • Dark mode
  • Excel-like copy, paste and fill down

Install in a minute

No administrator rights needed.

Download

Run the setup

Open GOLD Express

Safe by design

GOLD Express only does what you ask.

Reads GOLD - changes it only when you confirm

Everything is read-only, except the two bulk updates: they save only after you click Run update and confirm. Both have a Test-only mode.

Your data stays yours

The GOLD bot sends GOLD data only to answer your question - it is not used to train the AI and nothing is cached. Chats are not saved unless you choose to, and then only on your PC.

Questions

 

Windows says "Windows protected your PC" - is it safe?

Yes. The setup is not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows SmartScreen asks first. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Do I need to install updates myself?

No. GOLD Express checks for new versions and shows an update window - click Update now and it reopens on the new version in about a minute.

Does it change anything in GOLD?

Only when you run a bulk update (Revised Target Date or Bulk assignment) and confirm. Everything else - dashboards, overview, tracker, explorer, the GOLD bot - only reads GOLD.

Why does it need Firefox?

GOLD Express uses Firefox in the background to open GOLD with your Orange sign-in, the same way you would. You will not see it unless you turn it on in Settings.

Where are my files and settings?

Exports go to the folder set in Settings (Documents\GOLD Express\output by default). Settings and the DPM list stay on your PC.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > GOLD Express > Uninstall.
