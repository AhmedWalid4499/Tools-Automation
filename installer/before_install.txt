What you need
  - Mozilla Firefox installed on this computer.
    GOLD Express drives Firefox hidden in the background to work with GOLD.
  - Your normal GOLD access with your Orange account.
    Sign-in happens automatically (SSO). If GOLD ever asks you to sign in by hand,
    GOLD Express opens the Firefox window for you.
  - If the network drops, GOLD Express reconnects to GOLD by itself when it is back.
  - No administrator rights are needed - GOLD Express installs just for you.

What GOLD Express does
  - Overview: key facts of one or more orders (project, scope, STM, site, target
    dates, order value) with Red / Amber / Green delivery and 120-day lead-time
    status, exportable to Excel.
  - Order explorer: open any GOLD order, browse its tabs, take screenshots,
    download documents, see order teams and export tab data to Excel.
  - Revised Target Date: add the "Revised Target Date" milestone (Customer or
    Orange Business Services reason) to many orders at once from a table you
    type, paste from Excel or load from a file. "Test only" lets you try first
    without saving anything.
  - Bulk Order Assignment: make a DPM the primary STM of many orders at once,
    picked from the DPM Names list (which you can add to and edit).

Good to know
  - GOLD Express only changes GOLD when you click "Run update" on the
    Revised Target Date or Bulk Order Assignment tab and confirm.
    Everything else only reads GOLD.
  - Your settings, screenshots and exports are kept in Documents\GOLD Express.
    Uninstalling the app does not delete them.
